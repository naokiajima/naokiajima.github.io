Fun AI Logo Branding

Draws on a traditional concept of six circles, reimagined in a vibrant modern palette to reflect Fun AI's identity in a competitive industry — a visual declaration of resilience against the biggest players in AI.

Client
Functional AI Partners Pte Ltd
Industry
AI (AI Agent development)
Website
fun-ai.io
Tools
Illustrator

What's the idea behind the six circles?

Idea given by the client. THe client is Japanese and the story of six circles derived from a samurai called Sanada Yukimura. It was a reference to the Sanzu river in Buddhist mythology that in order to cross into the afterlife, one must pay six-coin toll to enter.

For Fun AI, this becomes a declaration: in an industry dominated by giants, the brand moves forward with the same unwavering commitment — protected, purposeful, and ready for whatever lies ahead.

Process

Firstly i determined the colors. Since it's about doing things the FUN way, i chose colors that represent youth, and exciting. Even though the colors were pastels, it's not too overly light. We don't consider black and white as colors.

With the six circles in mind, I did a lot of scribbling on papers before i started on my first few versions. In addition, client wants to add tagline "When Tech meets Knowledge". So i came out with first 3 versions as per shown below.

Conclusion? Yup, rejected. It's not what the client have in mind. He wants something close to the six circles. So, i went back to the original six coins design and came out something like this.



I personally feel this logo is nice but, the sequence is wrong. In logo design, icon must come first, followed by text (it's how i identify a brand. Visual first, text later). So i swapped the position.

Final Design

I shortened Functionalai Partners to Fun AI, so the proportion between the icon and the text don't differ too much. Oh, in the final logo, i omitted out the tagline. It was a little bit too busy.
