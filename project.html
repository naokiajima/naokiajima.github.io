Brand Website Redesign

A full redesign of a lifestyle brand's digital presence — focusing on creating a seamless, visually-led experience that reflects the brand's premium identity and improves conversion.

Client
Lifestyle Brand Co.
Role
UI/UX Designer
Year
2024
Tools

Cover Image

The Challenge

The existing website felt dated and didn't reflect the brand's premium positioning. Navigation was confusing, product pages lacked visual appeal, and mobile experience was an afterthought. Bounce rate was high and time-on-site was low.

The brief: redesign from the ground up with a clear visual identity, intuitive navigation, and a mobile-first approach that would reduce friction and increase engagement.

Process

I began with a thorough audit of the existing site, followed by competitive research and stakeholder interviews to understand both business goals and user needs. Wireframes were iterated quickly before moving into high-fidelity mockups.

After two rounds of stakeholder feedback, the visual direction was locked and I moved into building the full design system — typography scale, colour tokens, component library — before handing off to development.
