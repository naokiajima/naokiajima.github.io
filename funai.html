About Fun AI

Functional AI Partners or also known as Fun AI, was founded by Mr Nishida, a consultant guru with over twenty years of experience, noticed the game-changing potential of AI in reshaping the way we work.

Driven by the way AI can transform the daily grind, he utilized AI agents to handle the 'busy work,' shifting his full focus toward strategic productivity. This spark a vision in him.

My thought process

Since this is a brand new company, I had full authority of the visual outcome. Since from the start, I kind of affirmed that this website is going to look simple and functional. Clean with a lot of white spaces.

Same as other website, I listed down things that i want my audience to know in the homepage:

  • What the digital staff can do for you?
  • Digital Staff features
  • List of our digital staff
  • FAQ
  • Call to Action

What are the challenges faced?

It's my first time using Advanced Custom Field plugin. It wasn't much of a challenge, but a surprise to me. This plugin is so useful, especially setting up list of features/functions of each digital staff.

It's also my first time using loop grid template in Elementor. It was confusing at first but I got used to it eventually.

Other things I handled in FunAI beside website?

  • Fun AI held yearly internships for college students. I was in charge of interviewing and selecting 3 students from Singapore with computer science and business background.
  • Involved in testing of AI Agents such as Wei Tan and Julia X
