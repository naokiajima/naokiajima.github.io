About Hakubun

Originated from Japan, Hakubun strives to bring smile to children through development of effective educational learning materials for them to enjoy and easy for teachers to teach. They manufacture stem kits and educational products for Japanese primary schools.

My thought process

*As of today, the website is handled by Hakubun's team so whatever content displayed now, is not done by me.

Educational website for kids as target audience means fun and creative. Colors used must be striking and non serious. As this website focus heavily on each product, homepage doesn't have to be that complicated.

In product page, list all individual products. Each product has its own product name. Click into the product page, that shows:

  • Product Packaging visual
  • Product Video
  • What's inside the box
  • List of parts
  • Learning materials

What are the challenges faced?

Before putting content everything on the website, I have to manually change the learning materials, which was originally in Japanese, to English. Translations are provided. During initial products launch, some learning materials were ineditable, so I had to recreate some of them using Photoshop/Illustrator.
